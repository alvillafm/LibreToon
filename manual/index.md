# Welcome to the LibreToon Wiki

Learn to draw, animate and rig characters in **LibreToon**. This manual follows the **1.0.1** interface; experimental features and platform differences may affect the steps.

## New to animation?

Start with [Workspace and First Steps](getting-started.md), then follow [five short projects](learn/index.md). You will build a little owl, animate it, create a forest shot, add lighting, and rig a wing. Each lesson introduces a few tools and ends with a visible result.

## Already working on a project?

- [Drawing and Color](drawing.md): brushes, editable vectors, fills and palettes.
- [Timeline and Exposure Sheet](timeline.md): keys, exposures and tweens.
- [Symbols and Rigging](rigging.md): reusable artwork and character controls.
- [Cameras, Lights and FX](compositing.md): stage and compositing controls.
- [Saving and Recovery](saving.md): protect your editable work.
- [Import and Export](files.md): bring artwork in and deliver animation.
- [Performance](performance.md) and [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): diagnose slow or unexpected behavior.

Download the app from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/alvillafm/LibreToon/releases). Report reproducible problems through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/alvillafm/LibreToon/issues).

!!! note "Experimental software"
    Keep independent copies of important projects. Check a saved copy and a short export before committing to a long production session.
