# 1 · Draw a Little Owl

**Result:** a recognizable owl built from a handful of editable shapes. **Start:** a new 1280 × 720 project, 24 fps, with an unlocked vector layer.

## Build the silhouette

1. Open **Color** and choose **Fill**. Pick a medium brown. Choose **Stroke** and pick a darker brown.
2. Choose the ellipse tool on the left. Drag a tall oval near the center of the stage for the body.
3. Draw two small triangles with the polygon or Pen tool for ear tufts. Keep them simple; three corners are enough.
4. Draw two lighter ovals inside the body for the face patches.
5. Add two dark circular eyes and a small orange triangular beak. Use Selection to position the parts.
6. Draw a wing to one side as another oval. Keep this wing separate so you can rig it in lesson 5.

## Try an edit

Select a shape and change its fill. Then use Pen or Node to adjust one of its points. Undo once and redo once so you know how to recover from an accidental edit.

!!! success "Checkpoint"
    You can see the body, two eyes, beak and wing. Selecting or moving the wing does not move the entire owl. Save as `Owl-01-Drawing.ltoon`.

!!! tip "If paint leaks"
    Start with the closed primitive shapes in this lesson. For hand-drawn regions, use Paint Bucket gap controls cautiously and inspect the result; a large gap is often easier to close with a stroke first.
