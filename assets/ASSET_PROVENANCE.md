# LibreToon asset and development provenance

Recorded 2 November 2026. This record distinguishes the maintainer's declaration from repository observations. It is not a certificate of legal originality, ownership, non-infringement or absence of third-party rights, and it does not grant a new license.

## Maintainer declaration

The maintainer states that:
- LibreToon's own icons, graphics and visual elements were created with AI assistance through Codex.
- The LibreToon logo showing a blue mouse with the letters **LT** was generated through ChatGPT.
- LibreToon is directed by an animator and developed with AI assistance from an independent implementation in Rust. It is not a fork of Adobe Animate or another application.

These statements were supplied by the maintainer in this conversation on the date above. Specific model versions, prompts, generation dates and complete generation/editing histories have not been provided. They are not inferred from Git commit dates. This is a project provenance declaration, not an independent audit proving the origin of every line or pixel.

## Third-party visuals and dependencies — excluded from project authorship

- **NotoSansCJK-Regular.ttc** is a third-party font. Its inspected metadata declares © 2014–2021 Adobe. It retains the SIL OFL 1.1 notice in [fonts/OFL-NotoSansCJK.txt](fonts/OFL-NotoSansCJK.txt); see [font evidence](fonts/COPYRIGHTS.md). It is not a Codex/ChatGPT-created font or Adobe Animate program code.
- **epaint_default_fonts / egui fonts:** Hack/Bitstream Vera, Ubuntu, NotoEmoji, emoji-icon-font and egui-icons are third-party font/glyph resources. See [their notices](licenses/fonts/README.md). John Slegers' icon-font notice and the relevant MIT, OFL and Ubuntu font terms remain applicable. These glyphs are not reattributed to LibreToon.
- **User/system fonts** selected at runtime are supplied by their respective authors/platforms. This record does not grant rights to redistribute them or to users' imported artwork.
- **egui/eframe/epaint/egui-wgpu rendering and shader resources** belong to those dependencies and their contributors. They remain covered by their own terms and [dependency attribution](licenses/RUST-DEPENDENCIES.md), even when compiled into the program. Shader code from a dependency is not a project-created graphic merely because it renders LibreToon's interface.
- FFmpeg/Imageio and other libraries retain [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and their own licenses. They are excluded from the declaration of project-created visual assets.

The inspected assets directory contains the four application artwork files, the documented font, locale data and tool/licensing files; no separate bundled stock-image/icon-pack directory was identified by the file inventory. This is not an exhaustive rights audit of dependencies, source fragments, user content or earlier repository history.
